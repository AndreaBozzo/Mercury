# Code of conduct

Mercury's community spaces should be safe, professional, and focused on evidence.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith while still reviewing claims critically;
- welcome people regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint;
- critique work rather than people;
- protect private information and credentials;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- avoid hype, guaranteed-return claims, market manipulation, and pressure to enable unsafe
  execution.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where a
person represents the project.

Report conduct concerns privately using the route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may
edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a
participant. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, while recognizing that some
investigation may require limited disclosure.
